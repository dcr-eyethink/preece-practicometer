    Welcome to the Preece Practicometer!

    Along the menu bar are a set of functions to support practice. The chord grid randomises a chord sequence in a particular key. The circle of 5th button randomises a sequence of notes or chords to practice scales or chords. There are ear training functions for recognising notes and chords, and a library of scores and notes.

    You jump to the functions directly, or build up a practice list of these and other activities, setting a timer for each to keep your practice on track. All your practices are recorded in the log book.
